WELCOME TO THE SHOP

A good roll takes a little love.

  1. Order. Take the customer’s ticket. Each of the three rolls has its own recipe.
  2. Dough. Choose dough, flatten, butter, fill, roll, and slice. Drag tools across the board or use the action button. Cut when the marker reaches the green zone.
  3. Bake. Load all three rolls. Pull them out when the timer is in the green zone, around 20 seconds.
  4. Build. Select a roll and its icing, drizzle, and topping. Drag each ingredient onto the roll three times, or use the apply button.
  5. Serve. Match the ticket to earn stars and tips. Finish four customers to complete the day!

Switch stations at any time. The oven keeps cooking until you pause. Changing tabs automatically pauses the game. Use Tab and Enter for every action.
